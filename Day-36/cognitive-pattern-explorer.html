Cognitive Pattern Explorer

Explore How You Think 🧠

Cognitive Pattern Explorer is a reflective, educational space to notice how you approach everyday decisions — through gentle exercises, not tests. There's no scoring of you as a person, no diagnosis, and no right answer.

This app is for self-reflection and learning only. It does not diagnose, classify, or clinically assess mental health. If you're looking for support with your wellbeing, a licensed professional is the right resource.
