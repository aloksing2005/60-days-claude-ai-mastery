## What I Learned

1. AI decision systems should use real, citable data.
2. Different criteria can change the final ranking.
3. Weighting allows users to reflect their own priorities.
4. Transparent sources make AI-generated decisions easier to evaluate.
5. A good decision-support UI should explain the reasoning, not just show a winner.

## Key Insight

The "best" option can change depending on which criteria matter most to the decision-maker.
