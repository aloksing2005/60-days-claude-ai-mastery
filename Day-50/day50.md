# Day 50 — Defend Your Experience

## Project
Built an AI-powered adaptive interview defense simulator using Claude.

## Objective
The application challenges me to defend the claims and experiences mentioned in my resume through adaptive interview questions.

## My Interview Profile
- Target: Job / placement interviews
- Preparation: General interview practice
- Interviewer style: Tough, skeptical senior interviewer
- Resume focus: Internship, projects, technical skills and experience
- UI style: Bold & modern dark theme

## Key Experiences Tested
- Frontend Developer Internship at Code-A-Nova
- Alumni Association Platform
- Web development skills
- Academic and technical experience

## Key Learnings
1. Every resume claim should be backed by a clear explanation.
2. Interviewers can ask deeper follow-up questions about project decisions.
3. Quantifiable evidence makes experience more credible.
4. I should be prepared to explain my exact technical contribution.
5. Adaptive questioning is more useful than a fixed interview questionnaire.
