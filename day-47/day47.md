# Day 47 — Content Intelligence Studio

## 🚀 Project

Built an AI-powered Content Intelligence Studio using Claude.

The application analyzes content and provides AI-powered insights, scoring, recommendations, rewritten versions, and optimization opportunities.

## 🎯 What I Built

- AI Content Analysis
- Content Scoring
- Strength & Weakness Detection
- Missed Opportunity Detection
- Platform-Specific Recommendations
- Alternative Hooks
- Alternative Titles
- Content Rewriting
- Publishing Checklist
- Executive Summary
- Before vs After Comparison
- Content Health Report

## 🧠 What I Learned

1. AI can be used as a specialized content consultant rather than only a chatbot.
2. Different review stages can provide more useful content analysis.
3. Multimodal AI can analyze both written content and visual content.
4. AI recommendations can help improve content structure, hooks, and engagement.
5. A professional dashboard makes AI analysis easier to understand and act on.

## 💡 Key Insight

The most valuable lesson from Day 47 was that AI content analysis becomes more useful when feedback is structured into specific categories and actionable improvements instead of providing generic suggestions.
