# Marketing Investigation Report

## Selected Campaign
EcoBottle Launch Campaign (Fictional)

## Findings
- Budget heavily focused on awareness but lacked conversion strategy.
- Audience targeting was too broad.
- Customer feedback highlighted confusing messaging.
- Engagement was high but sales conversion remained low.

## Root Cause
Poor audience segmentation and unclear value proposition.

## Expert Recommendation
- Refine audience targeting.
- Simplify campaign messaging.
- Optimize landing pages.
- Run A/B tests for creatives and CTAs.

84/100
