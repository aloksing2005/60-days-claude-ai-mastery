# Day 45 — AI Decision Strategist

## 🎯 Challenge
Built an AI Decision Strategist using Claude to analyze a difficult career decision through structured decision-making.

## 🧠 Decision
I evaluated whether I should:

- Start applying for software development jobs now
- Continue DSA and project preparation in parallel
- Avoid waiting until I feel completely prepared

## 💡 Key Learning

The biggest insight I learned was that a decision does not always have to be treated as permanent.

Starting job applications now does not mean stopping preparation. I can apply while continuing to improve my DSA skills and projects.

## 🔍 What I Learned

1. Separate the actual decision from the general goal.
2. Identify assumptions before making a decision.
3. Consider reversibility when comparing options.
4. Use a decision matrix instead of relying only on emotions.
5. Think about risks before committing to an option.
6. A small experiment can provide useful information before making a bigger decision.

## 📊 Decision Framework

The Decision Strategist evaluated:

- Life/Career Upside
- Financial Safety
- Growth & Learning
- Stress Level
- Reversibility
- Long-term Alignment
- Regret Risk

## 🚀 Final Insight

My main takeaway is:

> I don't need to wait until I feel 100% ready before taking action. I can take a reversible step, learn from the result, and continue improving.
