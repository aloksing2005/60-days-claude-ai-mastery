Investing 101 · Learn before you start

You don't need to be rich to start investing

You said you're not investing yet but want to learn — this module explains the basics and lets you simulate outcomes before you commit real money.

Compounding

Interest earns interest. ₹1,000/month at 10% annual return becomes far more than ₹1,000 × months, because early contributions keep growing.

SIP / Index Funds

A Systematic Investment Plan lets you invest a fixed small amount regularly into a diversified fund — a common low-effort starting point.

Risk vs time horizon

The longer your money can stay invested, the more short-term ups and downs you can ride out. As a student, time is your biggest advantage.

Tips, Checklists & Resources

Keep building the habit

Money tips for students on stipend income

  • Track irregular income by date received, not by assumed monthly average.
  • Pay yourself first — move a fixed amount to savings the moment income lands.
  • Use student discounts and campus resources before paid alternatives.
  • Review subscriptions every semester — cancel what you haven't used in 30 days.
  • Round up spare change from expenses into your emergency fund goal.
